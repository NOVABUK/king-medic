Using Your NHIA or Oranghis Insurance

Getting treated with your insurance is simple.

1

Bring your card

Come with your NHIA or Oranghis card and a valid ID. Bring any referral or previous records too.

2

Register at the front desk

Our staff will confirm your enrolment and open your patient file in a few minutes.

3

See the doctor

Any tests or medications covered by your plan will be arranged for you.

Not sure what your plan covers? Ask us on WhatsApp.
