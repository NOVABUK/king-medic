Who we are

A Specialist Clinic Built Around Your Family's Health

Kings Medic & Diagnostics is a specialist clinic at the Pool of Bethesda in Akure. We combine experienced doctors, a modern laboratory and a warm, personal approach to care, so you do not have to go from place to place for answers.

We provide appropriate, compassionate and high-quality healthcare for seniors, children and the whole family. As an NHIA-accredited and registered Oranghis provider, we also make quality care affordable and easy to access.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you visit.

Which insurance plans do you accept?

We are NHIA accredited and a registered Oranghis provider. Please bring your card and a valid ID when you visit.

What should I bring for my first visit?

Bring your insurance card (if you are using one), a valid ID, and any referral letters or previous medical records you have.

How do I book an appointment?

Fill in the request form on our homepage, call us on +234 812 797 8318, or message us on WhatsApp. We will confirm your visit with you.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Do you offer laboratory tests?

Yes. Our modern in-house laboratory provides diagnostic testing so your doctor can get the answers they need quickly.

What should I do in an emergency?

Call our emergency line on +234 812 797 8318 straight away. In a life-threatening situation, get to the nearest emergency facility without delay.

Trusted source for health insights

Simple Health Tips From Our Clinic

General information to help you stay well. It does not replace advice from your doctor.

Prevention

Know Your Numbers

Regular blood pressure and blood sugar checks can pick up silent conditions early. Ask your doctor how often you should be checked.

Fever

Test Before You Treat

Not every fever is malaria. A quick laboratory test confirms the cause, so you get the right treatment instead of guessing.

Your visit

Prepare for Your Consultation

Note your symptoms and how long you have had them, list the medicines you take, and bring any earlier test results.
