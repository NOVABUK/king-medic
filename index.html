Using Your NHIA or Oranghis Insurance

Getting treated with your insurance is simple. Here is what to do.

1

Bring your card

Come with your NHIA or Oranghis card and a valid ID. If you have a referral or previous records, bring them too.

2

Register at the front desk

Our staff will confirm your enrolment and open your patient file. This takes only a few minutes.

3

See the doctor

You will be seen by a doctor, and any tests or medications covered by your plan will be arranged for you.
